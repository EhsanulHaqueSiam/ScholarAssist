# North America & China (Slate portals)

## 1. McCall MacBain Scholarships (McGill University)

**Deadline (international, incl. Bangladesh): 19 August 2026, 4:00 PM ET.** (Canada/US deadline is 23 Sep 2026 — Bangladeshi applicants studying at Canadian/US universities use that one.) Applications opened 1 June 2026.

### Documents required
- **Resume:** one page, PDF, minimum 10-point font, **no photos**; "high-impact summary" style.
- **Transcripts:** **unofficial** transcripts uploaded as PDF, in English or French. Non-English transcripts need a translation, combined into **one PDF** with the original. Official transcripts are NOT required for the scholarship application (McGill will require them separately for admission). You also self-report undergraduate GPA converted to a 4.0 scale, with an optional 50-word context note.
- **No photo, passport upload, or degree certificate** is listed for the scholarship application itself.
- Application form sections: personal info, up to 5 McGill programs of interest, activities (up to 4 community/campus activities, up to 3 intellectual/creative works, up to 5 co-op terms, up to 5 paid work experiences from the last 5 years).

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/resources/ , https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/faq/

### References — 2 required, plus verifiers
- **Academic reference** (professor familiar with your coursework; management-program applicants may substitute a professional reference, except Master of Management in Analytics which requires academic).
- **Community reference** (volunteer coordinator, employer, coach, mentor, university staff who knows your campus involvement). **Not** family, friends, or fellow students.
- **How:** you register them in the portal; they get an email link and fill an **online form** (English or French only). Email/mail submissions not accepted.
- **When: referee forms are due by the same application deadline (19 Aug 2026, 4 PM ET)** — not after. Chase your referees early.
- **Gotcha — verification contacts:** the application also requires **verifier contact details for one leadership experience and one volunteer role** (separate from your two referees).

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/faq/ , https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/resources/

### Essays / short answers
- **3 short answers, 105 characters each:** (1) an activity you used to do but no longer do, (2) two current goals outside academics, (3) three things not visible elsewhere in your application.
- **5 short essays:** community influence on education choices (225 words); why this McGill program + goals (225 words); a leadership experience (275 words, structured); a volunteer contribution (250 words); a significant "course correction"/pivot (250 words).
- **No video component.**

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/resources/

### English tests
- **NOT required at scholarship application time** (deadline is too early). Scores go to **McGill** with your program application later. Check the specific McGill program's English requirement (typically IELTS ~6.5–7.0 / TOEFL ~86–100; program-specific — UNVERIFIED per program). Bangladeshi degrees taught in English do not automatically exempt you at McGill — check McGill's exemption list (UNVERIFIED).
- GMAT/GRE etc. not required for the scholarship; optional to mention.

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/faq/

### The separate-admission trap
- **Yes — a completely separate McGill graduate program application is mandatory.** McGill applications open ~mid-September 2026 (i.e., **after** the scholarship deadline). Scholarship finalists must submit their McGill application **by December 2026 at the latest**, regardless of the program's own deadline. So: scholarship deadline comes first (Aug), McGill application second (Sep–Dec).
- **McGill application fee: CAD $139.63** (2026 cycle; covers a second program choice at no extra cost). Not covered/waived by the scholarship.

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/faq/ , https://www.mcgill.ca/gradapplicants/how-apply/you-apply-mcgill

### Eligibility fine print
One of: (a) on track to earn your **first bachelor's degree by August 2027**; (b) earned it **January 2021 or later**; (c) earned it earlier but were **age 30 or younger on 1 January 2026**. Must meet McGill's degree/language minimums. Part-time students may apply but must enroll full-time. **Max 2 lifetime applications**; can't reapply if you previously took a Regional Award or reached final interviews.

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/apply/ , https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/faq/

### Fees
No scholarship application fee. Only the McGill program fee above.

### Bangladesh-specific gotchas
- **University endorsement — the big one.** No Bangladeshi university (checked: Dhaka, BUET, BRAC, etc.) is on the ~90-university participating list (South Asia list = IIT Delhi, IIT Madras, Ashoka, University of Delhi, LUMS). Consequences:
  - Current students / graduates of 2024–2026: **must obtain endorsement from your university** (typically the VC/President/Provost's office).
  - Graduated 2023 or earlier from a non-participating institution: **endorsement still required regardless of graduation year**.
  - "Your application will not be considered without it." Institution-specific instructions appear when you start the application — start early; getting a Bangladeshi VC office to respond takes time.
- **Interviews:** regional interviews **Oct–Dec 2026** (~250 semi-finalists; notified ~2 weeks before; format/location for South Asia UNVERIFIED — likely virtual, not confirmed). Final interviews **March 2027 in Montreal** (~85 finalists, travel paid — you'd need a Canadian visitor visa from Bangladesh; embassy processing time is your problem, not theirs — UNVERIFIED whether they assist with visa letters).
- Consolation awards exist: Regional Award $10,000 CAD (international), Finalist Award $20,000 CAD renewable.
- No document attestation/embassy step at application stage.

Source: https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/apply/ , https://mccallmacbainscholars.org/faq/

## 2. Schwarzman Scholars (Tsinghua University, Beijing)

**Deadline (U.S./Global stream — Bangladeshis use this): 9 September 2026, 3:00 PM EDT.** "Late applications, even due to latency issues or last-minute emergencies, will not be accepted." **Submission is final — no reopening for edits.**

### Documents required
- **Resume/CV:** PDF or Word, **max 2 pages**.
- **Transcripts:** **scanned copy of an official transcript** for *each* undergraduate and graduate degree, showing full name, institution, degree conferred, conferral date. Screenshots/grade-portal PDFs rejected. **Non-English transcripts need certified English translation** (from the institution or a professional service). Files must not be password-protected. Study-abroad transcripts only if credits aren't on the main transcript. Hardcopy officials required upon enrollment.
- **Passport:** not uploaded, but you must enter legal name/nationality/DOB **exactly as on your passport**, and a valid passport matching your primary citizenship (for the China visa) is required if admitted. You can apply without one but they strongly advise having it first — Bangladeshi passport renewal queues make this worth doing early.
- **Video introduction:** 1 minute max, **highly recommended but optional**, hosted on YouTube or Vimeo as an unlisted, shareable, non-password-protected link.
- No photo upload mentioned.

Source: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/admissions/application-instructions/

### References — 3 required
- One **academic**, one addressing **leadership**, one additional. No family members/relatives.
- **How:** you trigger system-generated email invitations; recommenders submit via unique link through the online system only. You cannot upload letters. Non-English letters need translation attached.
- **When: all 3 letters must arrive by the applicant deadline (9 Sep 2026, 3 PM EDT).** No grace period. Status trackable on your Status page.

Source: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/admissions/application-instructions/ , https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/frequently-asked-questions/

### Essays / short answers
- **Leadership Essay: 750 words max.**
- **Statement of Purpose: 500 words max.**
- **2 short answers, 100 words each.**
- Word counts are strict — exceeding them **may disqualify** the application. Uploaded as PDF/Word. You must sign an acknowledgment that **no AI generator was used** in your written work.

Source: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/admissions/application-instructions/

### English tests
- **Required for Bangladeshi applicants unless** your undergraduate (or ≥2 years of a master's) was taught primarily **in English for at least 2 years** — if you can document 2+ years of English-medium instruction you're exempt (confirm how they verify — UNVERIFIED mechanism, likely transcript/letter).
- Minimums if not exempt: **TOEFL iBT 100, IELTS 7.0, Duolingo 130, Cambridge C1/C2 185.**
- Expiry: instructions note some exams are valid only 2 years — take/retake so the score is valid at the deadline.

Source: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/admissions/application-instructions/ , https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/frequently-asked-questions/

### Separate-admission trap
- **None.** Admission to Schwarzman Scholars = admission to the Master of Global Affairs at Tsinghua. One application, no separate university application, no fee.

### Eligibility fine print
- **Age: at least 18 and not yet 29 as of 1 August 2027** (born after 1 Aug 1998).
- Undergraduate degree fully completed **by 1 August 2027**.
- Bangladeshi citizens apply through the U.S./Global stream (Chinese passport holders have a separate January–May cycle). Special rules only if you were born in China or have Chinese-citizen parents.
- No work-experience requirement.

### Fees
**None.** No application fee; if selected, everything is covered (tuition, room/board, travel, insurance, stipend, laptop).

### Gotchas (incl. Bangladesh-specific)
- **Interviews are in-person and mandatory** (video only for rare exceptions like visa denial, at their discretion): **28–29 Oct 2026 in London; 4–5 Nov 2026 in New York.** You indicate your anticipated location in the application. **A Bangladeshi will need a UK or US visitor visa on ~2–3 weeks' notice** (interview invitations go out mid-October) — this is the single hardest logistical constraint; plan visa strategy before you apply. The program covers economy airfare/train + one hotel night.
- Interview format: ~25-min panel with 5–6 senior figures; at the ~20-minute mark you get a **random current-events question outside your field**.
- Dual nationals: passport in application must match the one used for the China student visa, or you're ineligible to enroll.
- Recommendation letters not included in your post-submission PDF proof — verify their arrival on the Status page yourself.
- No embassy attestation of documents required at application stage; Tsinghua's ISSO handles visa paperwork after acceptance.

Source: https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/admissions/application-instructions/ , https://www.schwarzmanscholars.org/frequently-asked-questions/

## 3. Knight-Hennessy Scholars (Stanford University)

**KHS deadline: 6 October 2026, 1:00 PM Pacific — same for all applicants regardless of program.**

### Documents required
- **Resume: strictly ONE page**, US Letter (8.5×11"), legible font, **no photos, no hyperlinks**, reverse-chronological.
- **Transcripts: unofficial is fine** — scan and upload for every institution attended ≥1 academic year (undergrad + grad). Must show **degree conferred + conferral date**; if not, also upload your **degree certificate/diploma**. A self-reported transcript form exists if you can't get an unofficial transcript. **Non-English transcripts and degree documents need official English translation.**
- No photo or passport upload required at application (visa invitation letters come only if you're named a finalist).

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/resume , https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/transcripts-test-scores

### References — 2 required
- Two recommenders from **academic, community, or professional** contexts. No family; peers allowed. Title/status irrelevant — evidence and anecdotes matter. Use work email addresses. You may reuse your Stanford-program recommenders, but KHS suggests considering different ones; letters must be tailored either way. Recommenders can't have you write/edit/translate the letter (you affirm this).
- **How:** register them in the online application early; system emails each a personalized link. They fill a rating grid + 5 prompts + letter.
- **When: due by the application deadline (6 Oct 2026)** — BUT you must submit your own application by the deadline even if letters are late; the system permits submission with letters outstanding, and late letters don't disqualify you ("do not delay submitting because you are waiting for a letter").

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/recommendation-letters

### Essays / short answers / video
- **3 short answers:** (1) short- and long-term academic/professional intentions — 250 words; (2) a time you a) engaged a different perspective, b) acted with courage, c) fell short of expectations — 250 words combined (one or several examples allowed); (3) **eight improbable facts** about you — 150 words combined.
- **1 essay:** "Connect the dots. How have the influences in your life shaped you?" — **550 words.**
- **Video statement — BY INVITATION ONLY (verified for 2027 cohort):** in January 2027, ~500 applicants are invited on a rolling basis; video due **5–10 days after invitation**; **max 2 minutes**; prompt: *"Teach something to your cohort of fellow Knight-Hennessy Scholars"*; **must be recorded inside the online application** (no uploads/external links); face must be visible; production value explicitly irrelevant. So no video at initial submission — but be ready to produce one on ~a week's notice in January.

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/short-answers-essay , https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/after-you-apply/video-statement-invitation

### English tests
- KHS itself has **no minimum**; it mirrors your Stanford program: if the program requires TOEFL/IELTS, you must also submit scores to KHS. All Stanford programs accept TOEFL; most accept IELTS; only the MBA accepts PTE. Typical Stanford minimums: **TOEFL 90 / IELTS 7.0** (program-specific). **Expiry: some departments accept scores only within 18 months, others 24 — check your program.** Scores can be added after KHS submission via the status page (due by the later of the program deadline or the KHS deadline).

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/transcripts-test-scores , https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/faqs-frequently-asked-questions

### The separate-admission trap (severe)
- **You must apply separately and concurrently to a full-time Stanford graduate program** (MA/MS/MBA/JD/MD/PhD, etc. — Honors Co-op, MLA, JSD, MLS, coterm excluded) and be admitted by it; **KHS cannot admit you to Stanford.** Committees review independently, but KHS repeatedly verifies with the program that you applied and "remain competitive."
- **Order of deadlines:** KHS first (6 Oct 2026), then your Stanford program application by **the earlier of the program's deadline or 1 December 2026**. Many Stanford PhD deadlines fall in early–mid December — for KHS you must beat 1 Dec anyway. **MBA applicants must use Round 1.** Submitting the program application after 1 Dec kills KHS eligibility (but not program admission).
- GRE/GMAT etc.: whatever the program requires, you need it; KHS wants those scores too.

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/application-deadlines , https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/after-you-apply/evaluation-processes

### Eligibility fine print
- **First bachelor's degree earned January 2020 or later** (military service: Jan 2018 or later). Current students eligible if finishing by September 2027. A later master's does NOT reset the clock.
- **No age limit, no field/university restrictions, no quotas, no endorsement needed.** All citizenships welcome. No deferral of a KHS offer — reapply instead.

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/before-you-apply/eligibility

### Fees
- **KHS application: free.**
- **Stanford graduate program application: US$125, nonrefundable** (GSB MBA has its own separate application and higher fee — amount UNVERIFIED). **KHS cannot waive program fees.** Stanford's waivers must be requested **≥10 business days before the program deadline** and before submitting. Most waiver routes are effectively unavailable to Bangladeshi applicants — budget the $125 (+ TOEFL/GRE score-sending fees).

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/online-application , https://gradadmissions.stanford.edu/apply/application-fee

### Gotchas (incl. Bangladesh-specific)
- **Timeline of later stages (2027 cohort, official):** video invitations rolling through January; decision-status update to ALL applicants **27 Jan 2027** (finalists notified same day); **online individual interview mid-February 2027**; **Immersion Weekend (in person at Stanford) 5–6 March 2027 — attendance mandatory** barring emergencies; scholars notified **16 March 2027**. A Bangladeshi finalist needs a **US B-1/B-2 visa within ~5 weeks** — KHS provides a visa invitation letter only after finalist selection. Dhaka US-embassy interview wait times can exceed this window; if you have any plausible reason to hold a valid US visa earlier, sort it before January (embassy timing UNVERIFIED — check current wait times).
- After submitting you CAN still: add test scores, change recommenders, change intended degree program, upload an updated resume (via status page). Everything else is locked.
- Stanford program admission decisions arrive anywhere December–April, independent of the KHS calendar — you can be a KHS finalist while still awaiting the program decision.
- Don't skimp on the form's background/employment sections just because it duplicates your resume — they explicitly evaluate it.
- KHS has an AI-use policy for application materials: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/preparing-your-applications/policy-ai-use (details UNVERIFIED).
- No document attestation/embassy steps at application stage; translations only.

Source: https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/after-you-apply/evaluation-processes , https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/admission/faqs-frequently-asked-questions

# United Kingdom

## 1. Chevening Scholarships (2027/2028 cycle)

### Portal and key dates
- Applications run on **ASAMS** (Applicant Scholar Alumni Management System) at **asams.chevening.org** — a system shared with the Marshall Scholarship, managed for the FCDO. Register at `asams.chevening.org/apply`.
- **Open:** Tuesday 4 August 2026. **Close: Tuesday 6 October 2026, 11:00 UTC (= 17:00 Bangladesh time)** — the system locks at that moment; no late access.
- Longlisting/reading committees: Oct 2026–Jan 2027. Shortlisting: mid-Feb 2027. **Interviews: March–April 2027 at British High Commission Dhaka**. Results from mid-June 2027.
- **Unconditional offer deadline: Thursday 8 July 2027, 17:00 BST** — you must submit at least one unconditional UK university offer by then.

Sources: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/ · https://asams.chevening.org/ · https://www.chevening.org/apply/

### Documents required
- **At application (by 6 Oct 2026):** essentially the form only — you do NOT upload references or education documents at this stage. You enter education and work history into the form and name two referees.
- **If shortlisted for interview:** upload to the online system **no later than 7 working days before your interview**: photo ID (passport), your **official degree certificate issued by your university (original documents required)**, and both reference letters. Documents can alternatively be submitted to the local Chevening team (British High Commission Dhaka).
- If you won't have education documents by the application deadline, you are still eligible — they're needed at the interview stage.
- **CV:** no CV upload; work history goes into the form's employment fields (UNVERIFIED for the new ASAMS form specifically).
- English translations/attestation standard for the degree certificate: UNVERIFIED; your chosen universities impose their own transcript rules for the admission side.

Sources: https://www.chevening.org/faqs/when-do-i-have-to-upload-all-of-my-supporting-documents/ · https://www.chevening.org/faqs/what-education-documents-do-i-need-to-upload/

### References
- **Two referees.** They may be **two professional, two academic, or one of each**.
- **Current position (verified from Chevening FAQs): you only provide referee names/details in the application. Reference letters are uploaded by YOU at the interview stage — at least 7 working days before your interview.** They are letters you collect, not portal submissions by the referees. Re-check inside ASAMS this cycle since the portal is new — UNVERIFIED whether ASAMS changes the mechanics.

Sources: https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/references/ · https://www.chevening.org/faqs/when-should-i-submit-references-in-support-of-my-application/

### Essays
Four essays, each **minimum 50 / maximum 500 words**, written in English:
1. **Leadership and influence** — evidence of your leadership/influencing skills.
2. **Networking** — examples of networking skill and how you'll engage the Chevening community.
3. **Studying in the UK** — why your three chosen courses; link to experience and plans.
4. **Career plan** — immediate plans on returning home + long-term goals.

The 50/500 limits are long-standing but re-check inside ASAMS (UNVERIFIED for the new portal specifically).

Source: https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/application-criteria/

### English tests
- **Chevening's own English language requirement was removed in 2020 and remains removed** — no IELTS needed for the Chevening application itself.
- BUT your universities' requirements still apply, and you must convert to an **unconditional** offer by 8 July 2027 — any university English condition (Bangladeshi degrees are rarely exempt) must clear well before July. Book IELTS early; this is the classic way Bangladeshi finalists lose the award.

Source: https://www.chevening.org/faqs/what-happened-to-chevenings-english-language-requirement/

### University offer mechanics
- You list **three different UK master's courses** (full-time, taught, starting autumn 2027) in the application; you apply to the universities **yourself, separately**.
- At least **one unconditional offer from one of your three listed courses** by 8 July 2027 17:00 BST. You cannot swap to a course not listed in your application.

### Eligibility fine print
- Citizen of a Chevening-eligible country (Bangladesh is) and **return to Bangladesh for a minimum of two years immediately after the award ends**.
- **Work experience: 2,800 hours minimum** (~2 years at 35 h/week, can be accumulated faster). Counts: full-time, part-time, voluntary work, paid/unpaid internships, freelancing, self-employment; before, during, or after your degree; any country; **up to 15 employment periods**. Does NOT count: work that was a mandatory part of your university course.
- **Ineligible:** British/dual-British citizens; employees, former employees (within 2 years) or **immediate relatives of employees** of HMG (incl. British High Commission), the British Council, a partner UK university; anyone who previously studied in the UK on a UK government-funded scholarship.

Sources: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/who-can-apply/work-experience/ · https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/eligibility/

### Fees
No Chevening application fee. UK universities' own application fees (£25–£100 typical) apply for your three course applications.

### Gotchas
- The 11:00 UTC deadline = **17:00 Bangladesh time**, not midnight.
- Original degree certificate needed at interview stage — if your university is slow issuing originals, start now. Provisional-certificate acceptability is country-team dependent — UNVERIFIED; ask the Dhaka Chevening team.
- **No deferrals:** selected candidates unable to take up the award must normally withdraw and reapply.
- Essays under 50 words or plagiarised/AI-flagged content voids the application (plagiarism warning is official; AI-detection specifics UNVERIFIED).

Sources: https://www.chevening.org/terms-and-conditions-chevening-bilateral-awards/ · https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/common-errors/

## 2. Commonwealth Master's and Commonwealth PhD (CSC), 2027/28

### Portal, routes, and key dates
- Applications run on **CSC Central** (the CSC accepts nothing outside it); navigate from cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk when applications open.
- **Opens Tuesday 8 September 2026; closes Tuesday 20 October 2026, 16:00 BST** (= 21:00 Bangladesh time), for study starting September/October 2027. Results ~July 2027.
- **The CSC does not accept direct applications — you must be nominated.** **Bangladesh's nominating agency is the University Grants Commission (UGC), Dhaka** (Joint Secretary, UGC Bhaban, Agargaon, Dhaka-1207). You apply on CSC Central **and** comply with UGC's own advertisement/process — **UGC's internal deadline is typically earlier than the CSC deadline** and its circular may require extra steps (hard copies, its own form). UGC's exact 2027 circular: UNVERIFIED — watch ugc.gov.bd from September 2026.
- **Commonwealth PhD (least developed countries and fragile states): Bangladesh is eligible.** Same portal, same nomination requirement, same closing date.
- Account registration uses **2FA** — you need a smartphone or second email, every login.

Sources: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarships/ · https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-phd-scholarships-for-least-developed-countries-and-fragile-states/ · https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply/national-nominating-agencies/

### Documents required (uploaded by YOU by 20 Oct 2026 16:00 BST)
- **Passport or national ID** showing photograph, date of birth, citizenship.
- **Full transcripts covering ALL higher education qualifications** — with certified translations if not in English.
- **References in PDF, signed** (see below).
- **PhD only:** a **supporting statement from a proposed supervisor at a UK university, signed, on institutional letterhead or as an email clearly showing the sender's details**. Getting a UK supervisor onboard before 20 Oct is the real PhD bottleneck — start contacting supervisors months ahead.
- No CV upload: education, up to **10 publications** (with DOIs), and up to **5 employments** (100 words each) go into form fields.

### References — the killer rule
- **At least two references, in PDF, signed, uploaded by the application closing date (20 Oct 2026, 16:00 BST). The CSC "is unable to accept references any other way or after this date." No exceptions, no late-referee grace.** An application without references in by the deadline is ineligible.
- The CSC's example form lists **three referees** — current employer, most recent academic study, and a **personal referee** (not related to you); prepare three (current-form count UNVERIFIED).
- CSC warns "a five-line reference is unlikely to provide the level of support necessary." It is **your responsibility** that each referee completes by the deadline.

Sources: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/advice-for-applicants/ · https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Example-application-form-Masters.pdf

### Form questions (from CSC's published example Master's form — limits may shift)
- **Development Impact statement in 4 parts:** relation to development issues + CSC theme (**200 words**); applying skills at home (**100**); expected change, outcomes, beneficiaries (**250**); how impact is measured (**100**). Plus a **CSC development theme** and up to **3 SDGs**.
- **Study outline** — 125 words; **Detailed plan of study** — 300 words; 3 keywords.
- **Career plans:** during award — 250; 5-year — 250; long-term — 250.
- **Personal statement** — 500 words; plus voluntary/leadership experience.
- Don't paste university brochure text (treated as plagiarism); don't centre personal success over development impact.

### English tests
- **"The CSC does not require any candidates to take an IELTS test."** Universities' own conditions may still apply to placement (handled during nomination — confirm with departments; UNVERIFIED).

### University list rules
- **Three UK universities/courses in order of preference**, all CSC part-funding-agreement institutions. **Preference order cannot be amended after submission**; CSC won't change university/course after selection except exceptionally. The form asks what contact you've had with the universities (having applied/been accepted is looked on favourably).

### Eligibility fine print
- Citizen of (or refugee in) an eligible Commonwealth country, permanently resident in one.
- Degree: **minimum upper second class (2:1)** standard, or 2:2 + relevant postgraduate qualification, by September 2027 (Bangladeshi CGPA equivalence: UNVERIFIED).
- **"Unable to afford to study in the UK without this scholarship"** — a declared criterion assessed in selection, **not a documented means test; no bank statements required by CSC** (whether UGC asks for any: UNVERIFIED).

### Fees

### Gotchas

## 3. Gates Cambridge (2027/28 entry)

### Single-form mechanics
- There is **no separate Gates Cambridge application**. You tick Gates Cambridge in the **funding section of the single Cambridge postgraduate application** (apply.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk). One form covers course admission + College membership + funding.
- **Your funding deadline = your course's deadline for the international round: Tuesday 8 December 2026 or Wednesday 6 January 2027 depending on the course** — check the Cambridge Course Directory. Some departments set course deadlines **earlier** — the earlier date wins. No post-submission edits.
- Opens September 2026. International round: shortlisting by early March 2027, **interviews 22–23 March 2027**, offers by early April, **72 hours to accept**.

Sources: https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/how-to-apply/ · https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/timeline/

### Documents required
- **Transcripts** from all prior institutions — uploaded by you by the deadline (certified originals typically an offer condition, not an application requirement — UNVERIFIED per course).
- Course-specific documents per the Course Directory (research proposal, writing samples, CV in the course's format).
- **PhD applicants: a research proposal is required for Gates funding** even where the course itself doesn't demand one.

### References
- **Two academic references** — submitted **by the referees via the Referee Portal, by your funding deadline** (23:59 GMT). You may submit before they do, but funding consideration requires the complete application including references by the deadline. Chasing referees is your responsibility.
- **Plus one Gates Cambridge reference** — a personal/leadership reference from someone "of appropriate standing" (**not friends or family**). One academic referee MAY double as the Gates referee but must write **two distinct references**.
- **A Gates referee missing the deadline silently drops you from funding consideration** even though the admission application survives.

Sources: https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/how-to-apply/ · https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/apply/how/references

### Statement rules
Four short questions mapped to the selection criteria (academic excellence; course fit with future plans; commitment to improving the lives of others; leadership), each roughly **200 words / ~1,400 characters** (per-question caps UNVERIFIED beyond the academic-excellence question).

### English tests
- Gates sets no requirement of its own; **Cambridge's course-level requirement applies**. A Bangladeshi bachelor's does **not** exempt you. IELTS Academic / TOEFL iBT, **all scores in one sitting, dated within 2 years of course start**; typically IELTS 7.0–7.5 (per course). Usually an **offer condition**, EXCEPT **LLM and MCL, which require evidence at application**. TOEFL taken after 21 Jan 2026 triggers an extra Cambridge Language Centre online assessment.

Source: https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/apply/before/english-language-requirements

### Eligibility fine print
- Open to citizens of any country other than the UK. Eligible: full/part-time PhD, MLitt, or a one-year full-time postgraduate course. NOT eligible: PGCert, PGDip, MSt, MASt, MBA, EMBA, MFin, BusD, EdD, PGCE, MD, and a few others. Part-time master's ineligible.

### Fees — good news for Bangladesh
- Cambridge application fee: **£85 (master's) / £20 (doctoral)** — BUT **Bangladesh is on Cambridge's fee-waiver list: nationals who have lived there since birth pay NO application fee, waived automatically at submission.** (Significant time abroad may disqualify the automatic waiver.)

Sources: https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/apply/how/application-fee · https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/apply/how/application-fee-waiver

### Gotchas
- **One shot per course, no post-submission edits** — Gates answers and references freeze at submission.
- The **72-hour acceptance window** after an offer (early April 2027) — be reachable.
- Admission and funding are assessed by different bodies, but failing admission kills the scholarship regardless of Gates merit.
- Deferral: no published policy — UNVERIFIED; treat as unavailable.

6. **Transcripts** — all university studies including unfinished ones
7. **English proof** (see below)
8. **Two Letters of Recommendation** (see below)
9. **Essay** — mandatory template with fixed topic/length, signed AI declaration

### References
Exactly **two**; applicant uploads them (referees must NOT send directly — opposite of DAAD). Must be scanned originals with date, referee contact details, preferably letterhead/stamp. **Signature must be handwritten or a qualified e-signature tool (DocuSign/Adobe Sign); a signature image pasted into a document = automatic formal rejection.** At least one academic referee recommended.

### Motivation letter / essay
Both are template-locked (topic and length defined inside the Word templates — download from marihe.eu Application Documents). Undeclared AI use = formal rejection; ghost-writing banned. Round 2 video: **max 2 minutes, ≤10 MB**, self-intro + motivation + "why would you select yourself" (max 3 points), usually within 10 days of notice.

### English tests
- IELTS Academic **6.5**, no band below 5.5
- TOEFL iBT 92 (old scale) / 4.5 avg, no section <4 (new scale from 21 Jan 2026)
- PTE 62; Cambridge C1 Advanced grade C / C2 grade C1
- **MOI NOT accepted for Bangladesh.** Exemption only for degrees completed in listed English-speaking/EU countries; "completing previous studies in English" at a Bangladeshi university does not qualify. Duolingo, university letters explicitly rejected.
- Late IELTS allowed to **11 Dec 2026** with signed statement.

### Work experience
**Not an admission requirement** (strengthens application if in higher-education sector).

### Fees & funding
**No application fee.** Two outcomes: Tuition Fee scholarship (EUR 18,000 participation cost waived) or full Erasmus Mundus scholarship; you choose to be considered for either or both, and EM consideration doesn't hurt the tuition-waiver track. EM scholarship amount: see Costs page (UNVERIFIED exact monthly figure this cycle).

### Gotchas
- The **11 Dec 2026 hard document deadline** for late degree/IELTS is separate from the 21 Sep application deadline.
- Pasted-image signatures on recommendation letters are the most common formal kill.
- No photo in CV; ID and test certificate reviewed only at eligibility check (anti-bias).
- Only the FIRST degree counts for eligibility — you can't combine degrees or substitute work experience.

### Documents (all uploaded online, max 4 MB/file, NO hard copies to Tempus)

**Translations:** required only if documents aren't in the programme language/Hungarian (transcripts: or English). Certified translation expected for non-English originals. **No notarization/apostille required at application stage** — simple scans of originals suffice (host institutions may require legalized documents at enrollment: UNVERIFIED per-university).
