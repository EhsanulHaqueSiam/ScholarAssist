## Structural notes

- **Slate cluster:** McCall MacBain, Schwarzman, Knight-Hennessy, and Fulbright/IIE all run on Technolutions Slate with byte-identical registration forms (verified live). One essay bank and one recommender roster covers the first three; ScholarAssist ships one config per portal reusing the same field structure.
- **Nomination-gated** (Commonwealth, CSC China, MEXT, Eiffel, JJ/WBGSP, Pearson): a hidden earlier deadline belongs to the nominating body. For both Commonwealth AND Stipendium Hungaricum, Bangladesh's agency is the Secondary and Higher Education Division, Ministry of Education (shed.gov.bd); its Commonwealth circular lands before CSC's 20 Oct portal deadline. Budget 4 to 8 extra weeks. Eiffel goes further: direct student applications are declared ineligible; a French institution must nominate you.
- **Two-application programs** (McCall MacBain, Chevening, Gates Cambridge, Clarendon, SISGP): the university admission application is separate and often earlier.
- **Chevening moved portals:** apply.chevening.org no longer resolves and chevening.tal.net is retired; the live 2027/28 portal is **asams.chevening.org**.
- **Next publication waves:** Sep-Oct 2026 (GKS-U, Erasmus Mundus consortia, HKPFS) and Jan-Feb 2027 (Türkiye, Hungary, Sweden, Australia Awards, Romania, Ireland, ICCR, Rotary). Everything UNVERIFIED above should re-checked then.

## Using this with ScholarAssist

```sh
scholar track list          # the 10 verified deadlines are already tracked
scholar configs list        # 12 portal configs, structures captured live
scholar run chevening --flow apply          # starts the ASAMS award chooser
scholar run mccall-macbain --flow register  # fills the Slate account form, you confirm
```

Application forms behind logins are recorded once you have accounts: `scholar config record <portal-url>`.
